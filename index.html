How to play
Missions
You don't steer anyone around. Open Missions (O) and hand out jobs: your hero explores, takes camps, defends the town or gathers; generals lead your troops; the Builders' Guild puts up buildings. Everyone reports back when done.
Look
WASD, arrow keys or right-drag to look around, wheel or +/− to zoom. Space returns to your town.Drag to look around, pinch to zoom. Home returns to your town.
Explore
Scouts roam the fog on their own, bring back treasure and flee from danger. You start with one; send more from the Town Hall or with X. Your hero can explore too.
Select
Tap any building, unit, camp or resource to see what it does and act on it
War
Tap an enemy camp and send your hero or a general. Generals take the town guard with them as their warband, take the camp and march home. Hire generals at a Barracks. Drag to box-select troops; Q selects them all.Hold a finger, then drag, to box-select troops.
Your hero
Takes one mission at a time, fights back, goes home to heal when hurt and levels up. To send them somewhere yourself, select them (E) and tap the map. ZTheir Whirlwind hits everything close by. Troops can follow the hero as a warband of up to 12: use Follow the hero on a soldier, or Take the town guard on the hero.
Villagers
Hire them at the Town Hall (H) and set their jobs. They gather and farm for you.
Goals
Follow the goal under the resource bar for rewards; tap it to read it in full.
Grow
Research in the tech tree (R): each technology makes the next dearer, and the later ones need a bigger Town Hall, so choose. Builders upgrade buildings, but none can outgrow the Town Hall, and the hall needs two taken camps to become a castle (level 3). FThe 1× button changes game speed.
Build
Hire the Builders' Guild at the Town Hall. Then order buildings on the Missions board and the builders pick the spot, or choose one with keys 1–0 or the bar below and clickwith Build and tap a green square inside your blue border. Builders put it up. Storehouses and Granaries add storage, a Market trades, a Blacksmith forges arms for research and a Temple heals. Spike traps on the raiders' road hurt and slow them.
Resources run out
Forests, hills and gold veins are used up, whether villagers gather them or a Lumber Camp, Quarry or Mine draws on them; then build a new one by fresh ground, or take camps for more land. Farms wear out and are re-sown with wood. Your stores only hold so much, and anything more goes to waste.
Train
Soldiers (T) and generals come from the Barracks, archers (Y) from an Archery Range, knights (U) from a Stable and catapults from a Siege Workshop. Bigger ones train faster.
Defend
Troops that are not with a general guard the town. Towers and the Town Hall shoot raiders. A hero or general on a defend mission meets them on the road, and one waiting in town fights them at the gate.
Know the enemy
Every camp is a brute warband, a slinger nest, a rider camp or a shield fort, and its raids are the same. Archers cut down brutes but bounce off shields; knights ride down slingers and break shield walls; soldiers' spears stop riders, which otherwise run down archers and villagers. Before you send anyone, the camp shows a battle forecast; a general takes the troops that fight that camp best.
Upkeep
Everyone eats, and soldiers and generals want wages. Without food your people starve; without gold your soldiers desert. The food and gold chips show what you gain or lose each second. Farms, gold mines, a Market and taken camps pay for a bigger army.
The council
Most mornings brings a decision with no free answer: pay tribute or face a bigger raid, hire sellswords, dig wells, pay healers, and more.
Conquer
Break a red camp to claim its land and loot. Take them all to win. Raiders come for your Town Hall after the first nights, with battering rams from day 4 on Normal. On day 10 (Normal) the warlord's horde marches, and keeps coming every three days until you take the fortress.
Sound
M or the The Sound button turns effects on and off
