Day 1

Development tree

SHARDLANDS

Lift the fog. Raise a realm. Take every red camp.

Move
WASD or arrow keys, or tap the ground
Explore
X or Auto-explore: your hero scouts the fog and grabs treasure, avoiding camps
Gather
Space next to trees, rocks or gold, or tap them
Select
Tap any building, unit, camp or resource to see what it does and act on it
Command
With units selected, tap the map to move them or an enemy to attack. Drag (or hold, then drag on a phone) to box-select. Q selects the army, E the hero.
Camera
Drag with one finger or the right mouse button to look around. E or the Hero button brings it back.
Villagers
Hire them at the Town Hall (H) and set their jobs. They gather and farm for you.
Grow
Research in the tech tree (R), upgrade buildings to level 3, level up your hero. F changes game speed.
Build
Keys 1–8 or the bar below, then click a green square inside your blue border
Train
T soldier, Y archer, U knight. Needs a Barracks. Troops follow you.
Fight
Walk up to enemies or tap them. Your hero attacks on its own.
Conquer
Break a red camp to claim its land and loot. Take them all to win. Raiders come for your Town Hall after the first nights.
